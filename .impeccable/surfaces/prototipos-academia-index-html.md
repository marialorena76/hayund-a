## Direction contract

THESIS: The academy is a study plan with correlativities: one map shows how every offer connects, from first step to specialization, and the visitor's profile highlights her own path. It refuses the category default of a photo hero over a grid of equal pastel course cards.

OWN-WORLD: Official study-plan paper: cool off-white ruled sheet (#F2F3EC), ink-blue (#1F2A5C) for type, rules and connectors, a yellow-green highlighter (#E9F36A) that marks the visitor's path, rubber-stamp red (#B0284A) and green for status stamps, hairline frames, faint graph grid behind the map. Archivo (expanded, for plan headings and data), Source Serif 4 for reading, Caveat for Adriana's margin notes. Ave Mujer terracotta only as a mark on community cards.

STORY: The visitor understands that the school is a path, sees where she stands, and finds every option with its requirement, modality, dates, prices for Argentina and abroad, and a way to enroll.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A framed document header (Plan de estudios · Escuela de Memoria Celular Hay un Día · Rosario · Ciclo 2026–2027), H1 and Adriana's one-line promise; the "¿Por dónde empiezo?" three-option profile selector; the correlativities map across four columns (Primeros pasos · Formación · Especialización · Comunidad profesional) with connectors. Choosing a profile draws highlighter strokes along the path and marks the recommended cards below.
